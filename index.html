Our Heritage Story

50 Years of Sweetening Kanpur's Elite

Established in 1972 in the cultural core of Kanpur, we started with a singular philosophy: to elevate traditional Indian sweets into high art. By combining milk solids, pure silver foil, and cow-ghee base, we established a legacy of taste.

Every detail of our preparation remains faithful to ancestral hand-kneaded recipes, handled by master sweet artisans whose families have worked alongside ours for generations.
