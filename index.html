Our Heritage Story

50 Years of Sweetening Kanpur's Celebrations

Established in 1972 at the heart of Kanpur, our family-owned sweet shop started with a simple vision: to make the most authentic, rich, and high-quality traditional sweets using only 100% pure cow ghee.

Over the decades, we have maintained the exact same recipes passed down through generations. Our master karigars (artisans) take pride in hand-crafting each batch, ensuring the unique taste and quality that Kanpurites know and love.
